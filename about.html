WHO WE ARE

Our Story

Over a decade ago, our founders found themselves trapped by an unpredicted September blizzard on an exposed ridge above 12,000 feet. When cheap seams sheared and fiberglass poles splintered in the gale, a fundamental truth became clear: standard gear was built for showrooms, not real wilderness survival.

Returning to Boulder, they gathered master pattern-makers, aerospace metallurgists, and veteran mountain guides to craft gear obsessive about structural integrity, featherlight portability, and ethical sourcing. Today, Stackly is trusted across polar traverses, deep canyon explorations, and peaceful family weekends.
